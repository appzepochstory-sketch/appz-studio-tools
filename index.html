appz Studio Tools

A private, single-user tool for organizing the operator's own Pinterest account

What this is

A private tool used by appz (illustrator, Tokyo / @appz_yuto) to organize their own Pinterest account @appz2. It is not a product and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Reads the operator's own Pins and Boards to build an inventory (title, description, board, analytics)
  • Proposes better titles, descriptions and board assignments so the operator's illustration work is discoverable in search, particularly outside Japan
  • Applies only the changes the operator has individually reviewed and approved — nothing is published automatically

Scope

It accesses the operator's own account only. It does not read or write other users' data and does not act on behalf of anyone else.
